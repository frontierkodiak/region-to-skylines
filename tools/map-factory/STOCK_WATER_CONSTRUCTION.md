# Cut-only stock-style trial

This replaces PR99's raised banks and circular source pools. It is an
engineering proposal, not a stable-water guarantee. PR99 remains draft.

The uncarved game-metre ground is the input. PL1329's vertical transform
belongs before this stage; it is not enabled in this comparison. Water
authoring only cuts terrain. Existing transport grading remains separate.

- Mapped broad rivers use a 192 m section and up to 40 m depth; line-only
  side channels use 64 m and 16 m. River-area interiors are retained.
  Surface caps sample the untouched exterior channel rim, not unrelated
  low ground beyond it or the bed of another mapped water body.
- Large mapped lakes are deepened in place. Bodies below 54 native nodes
  remain ground/marsh, without an automatic pool. Perennial mapped streams
  use the narrow channel, not a series of pools. Explicit intermittent
  reaches remain unforced. A disconnected reach without an edge/sea outlet
  receives no invented supply; this limitation remains in its port report.
- River feeds sit at least 128 m inside mapped entry reaches, and at captured
  inland endpoints whose authored stage descends into the channel. These
  are engineered feeds, not claims of geographic springs. One inland
  high point is used if no inlet was found. There is no
  regulator at each river node and no automatic starting reservoir.
- A local D8 catchment scales the experimental source setting, capped at
  32767/65535; line-only feeds are capped at 655/65535. These are game
  settings, not measured discharge. Off-square catchment remains unknown.
- The captured coastal level is preserved. Inland engine sea level uses
  the lowest constructed edge outlet. This is an explicit receiving-stage
  hypothesis, not a claim that sea level caused the old failures.

Saved stock data show wide variation in settings, source pools and target
heights. They do not support a universal low-rate-lake/high-rate-river rule.
The chosen dimensions and settings need the requested native comparison.

Test Asheville and Pittsburgh first, against checks calibrated on the three
fixed stock maps and two unchanged shipping exports. Keep all original
geographic probes and raw historical outcomes. Only proceed to the other
six fixed development squares if both first trials pass. No holdout claim
or qualification is transferred from stock maps or older packages.
