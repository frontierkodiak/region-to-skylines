# Continuous fed-channel trial

This is a general receiving-path construction experiment, not a safe-water class
or deployment approval. PR107 and PR110 remain unchanged drafts. Every original
geographic point remains scored, including deliberate dry/marsh choices.

## Construction

Use original geometry and pre-water terrain, not native wet outcomes. Existing
mapped channels/areas touching the edge or ocean are potential receiving networks.
An isolated mapped reach can join one only along deterministic D8 terrain drainage,
at most 2048 m and within 128 m of captured water geometry. An arbitrary new map
edge is not an outlet. Unsupported reaches stay ground/marsh.

A lowest-spill, four-adjacent tree over the physical channel domain carries local
bank-stage ceilings downstream before recutting the bed. Every selected tree edge
has a non-climbing path to its receiver. Junctions share physical nodes/levels;
this engineered potential is not known geographic flow or outlet capacity.
The previous 64/192 m cross sections, 16/40 m depths and catchment-setting formula
are unchanged. No bank raising, terrain-drama transform, rate sweep or per-node
stream regulators. These dimensions/settings are not surveyed widths or discharge.
Qualifying isolated mapped lakes retain their own level source. Every residual
unfed authored wet component is restored to pre-water terrain and zero depth.
