## The new construction and its limits

The water stage only cuts. It does not raise banks or make PR99's circular
source pools. Mapped broad rivers use a 192 m / 40 m section; minor lines use
64 m / 16 m. Larger mapped lakes are deepened in place; bodies below 54 nodes
remain ground/marsh. All perennial captured streams remain in the channel and
probe inventories. A local D8 catchment scales experimental entry/head-feed
settings; it is not the off-map watershed or a measured supply budget.

Asheville has 274 controls; Pittsburgh 122. These counts are not asserted to
match stock maps with different source geometry. Feeds are at least 128 m
inboard. A disconnected component without a receiving edge/sea gets no source:
that is a real unfinished part of this construction, not a solved outlet.
No per-node regulators, automatic starting reservoir or new admission system
was added. Existing transport grading is separate from the no-fill water stage.

All eight authored packages are byte-identical between CPython and the browser's
Pyodide path. That proves the same writer runs, not that six unrun maps work.
Historical fixture tests use the retained writer snapshot, not an invented
native qualification for these new packages.

## Failure distribution and next engineering step

The tested premise was: deeper cut-only beds plus hand-initialized water and
head feeds are enough for stable water. The full first-hour traces reject it.
Main rivers persist, but tributaries and initialized bodies lose water; there
is also flooding beyond the reference margin. More depth alone is not a fix.

- asheville: 1527/1634 final nearby-dry original points lie outside an initial water component containing a source; 107 lie in sourced components.
- pittsburgh: 1021/1119 final nearby-dry original points lie outside an initial water component containing a source; 98 lie in sourced components.

This exact-source-cell/initial-raster classification is descriptive. It does not
establish engine hydraulic communication or an exclusive cause. All original
points were classified, not only selected failures. Flooding and surface
movement remain independent failures.

**Recommendation:** replace the unresolved tributary-outlet skip with a complete
catchment-to-receiver construction before adding more water. Every proposed
branch needs a continuous receiving path and a supported feed; increasing the
depth of disconnected hand-filled channels repeats the failed premise. Preserve
the same geography and checks for the next implementation, rather than omitting
dry reaches or changing this result into a pass.
