# Functional supply and receiving controls

This revises the gameplay-water constructor after the v6 Asheville hour failed flooding and retention. Earlier outcomes are known. It is a fresh construction rule, not a source-rate sweep or a reporting retry. The same eight squares, captured geometry, inputs and original probes remain fixed.

## Construction

- Form the complete authored water footprint from every retained surface waterway/body, width and emitting-source pool. The original geometry selects the domain, never a native wet/dry result.
- Before the joint surface solve, find components with no emitting control. Give each one a source-derived head inlet, using map-local catchment and the practitioner's 0.01 starting setting. Keep existing emitting controls as a prefix. Apply the existing emission-pool, underground-gap and depth rules; do not borrow a neighbouring component's supply merely because it is close.
- After the final footprint/profile solve, assign a nonemitting receiving control to every final component independently of emitter-spacing rules. Use its actual final receiving-port namespace, not the earlier centreline tree's component indices.
- The receiving control is anchored in existing authored water at least 1 m deep, near the intended root, with at least 1 m remaining above its unchanged bed at that root's intended surface. A map-edge/sea port stays a map-edge/sea design. A closed capture receives an explicitly engineered drain; this does not invent a geographic underground river or claim that its captured geometry reaches the sea.
- Split the sum of declared emitting **game settings** into uint16 intake records as needed. This is not a calibrated flux, measured outlet capacity or boundary-condition proof. Implicit boundary exchange and transient stored water remain separate.
- Receiving controls have output zero. They do not widen/deepen terrain to fit an emission disk. Partial wet intake support is explicit; this grants no permission for an emitter to add water outside its qualified pool. Original terrain/water contracts and new exact-depth engineered anchor probes are retained.

Map-makers use deliberate outlets and submerged sink controls: [Maestro and Jake's first-person accounts](https://steamcommunity.com/app/255710/discussions/0/611701999536523575/). Those accounts motivate the operation, not our numeric capacity conversion.
