cell W=0. Declaring its two source features one system does not change D=0.
It cannot satisfy the model's positive-depth role. A new group identifier cannot
remove that contradiction or turn a geometry contact into hydraulic authority.

The primary acceptance check is different: each original natural contract needs
95% nearby water >0.1m within64m, including initialization. It does not require

that all possible designs or these geographies are impossible. Phase29's complete
startup obstructions remain separately bound evidence, not a new rescore here.

Nor can a joint water-ownership rule remove infrastructure identity or local bank
