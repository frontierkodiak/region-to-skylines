# Literal exterior open-face operation model v2

V1 exact operation outputs and all earlier geometry/results are known and retained.
This is a NEW model, not an edit/reuse of v1. Commit method/tests/policy, then freeze
complete old cases/contracts/physical/QLDpoint data and v1outputs before new outputs.
All five fixed cases plus coast remain; no native or physical serialization.

Only old profiles unresolved because their full bank extends outside the square
are reconsidered. A literal clipped source endpoint at exactly x/z=+-8640 can
declare the corresponding OUTSIDE neighbours as an open port, using only that
node's own source aliases. Rounded-near-edge, other raster-coincident pieces and
names cannot lend a port. Unknown exterior lateral nodes remain unresolved.

EVERY in-square eight-neighbour bank remains untouched and supplies the same
>=.5m freeboard cap; no in-square node is removed or reclassified by QLD inclusion.
At least one in-square bank witness is required per node. Source/numeric bounds
remain max1.5m/olddepth, nofill/cut<=3m and greatest nonincreasing integer stages.
Cycles/conflicting same-cell potentials remain refusals. No arbitrary positive
grade, closed-pond map-edge escape ceiling or per-node regulator fallback.

The exterior opening is an explicit geometric port PROPOSAL, not measured boundary
stage/capacity, hydraulic connection or admission. All old refusal reasons,
protection masks/buffers/control bytes and source branches/probes remain. Exact
operation checks from v1 still apply, including source-space bank/freeboard and
no protected edits. Profiles with other old statuses are reused without resolve;
this is not another unchanged full stage sweep or source-provider hunt.
