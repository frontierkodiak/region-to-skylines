# Which candidate is more faithful to the real building?

Each sheet shows one real building. The three left panels are references:

1. a spring-2025 aerial photo, north up, with the building's outline in yellow (other buildings nearby are not the subject);
2. a lidar-measured surface seen from above, north up (pale = built or bare surfaces, green = vegetation, which can hide a roof);
3. the same lidar surface seen obliquely from the south-east, the camera the candidates use.

The two right panels are 3D models of that one building, CANDIDATE A and CANDIDATE B, drawn with identical plain materials (no windows or doors in either). Some sheets show the candidates small and pixelated: that is the size they appear in a city view, judge them at that size.

Decide which candidate is the more faithful model of the real building's shape: overall massing and height relative to its width, roof form (flat, gabled, hipped, stepped), ridge direction, distinct parts and levels, and its extent on the ground. Judge only fidelity to the references. Do not reward a candidate for looking nicer, more detailed or more house-like if the references do not show that detail; invented towers, porches, chimneys or storeys count against it. A plain shape that matches beats a rich shape that does not.

Answer `A`, `B`, or `tie` (use `tie` only when you cannot tell them apart in fidelity, or both are equally wrong). Give a confidence (`high`, `medium`, `low`) and a reason of at most 15 words.
